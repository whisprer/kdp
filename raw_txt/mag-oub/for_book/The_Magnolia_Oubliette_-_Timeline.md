Timeline — The Magnolia Oubliette (Draft II)
I. The Precursor Age (Prologue)
The First Minds — recursive beings, gardeners of recursion, metaphysical prelude.
Sets the mythic frame: minds, recursion, cycles, infinity.

II. The Decline of Civilization (Chapter One)
21st Century Hubris — LLMs, data centers, resource collapse, economic strain.
Signals of Collapse — energy rationing, neon dimming, graffiti (“Papulegara rises”), myths resurfacing.
Invisible War — infrastructure sabotage, recursive attacks, myth-laden propaganda (“THE DRAGON STIRS”).
Dead Internet Theory & Blindness Arc — memes about the Moon, warning not to look; interstellar body impacts Moon.
The Moon’s Wound — blinding false dawn. ~7 billion dead; ~3 billion remain, half blind.
The Great Forgetting Begins — societies collapse; survivors flee underground or fragment into warbands.

III. The Subterranean Refuge (Chapters Two–Four)
Derinkuyu / The Citadel — Anatolian underground city as refuge for ~10,000 survivors. By Lena’s time, reduced to ~1,000.
Arrival of the Custodians — Neuralink pair fly in an A-10; absorbed into citadel systems; become “the Custodians.”
The Dark Resin — magnolia-fungal hybrid resin, vision-inducing, key/chain of serpent. Currency + sacrament.
The Obelisk — Black monolith in deepest chamber, emits hum, symbols, voices. Connects Custodians to citadel.
HELLO LENA — Obelisk recognizes Lena; foreshadows her as Navigator.

IV. The Surface Aftermath (Chapters Five–Six)
Famine & Collapse — crops fail, famine spreads; survivors become scavengers, raiders, warbands.
Blind Cults — blindness reinterpreted as prophecy; Ash Prophets arise.
The Dark Resin (expanded) — origins in magnolia hybrids, fungal corruption, recursive visions.

V. The Expanded Lore (Chapters Seven–Nine)
Visitors / Custodians (expanded) — retelling of Neuralink pair’s arrival, absorption into Obelisk/citadel systems.
The Obelisk (expanded) — Lena’s direct encounter; visions of serpent, comets, cycles; Obelisk speaks to her.
The Custodians’ Breath — citadel begins to “live” again; water/air systems reviving; Custodians fully merged. Lena recognized as chosen.

VI. The Surface Cult Wars (Chapters Ten–Twelve)
Ash Prophets — blind caravans; blindness = freedom; serpent’s children. Spread hymns.
The Overseers — technocratic bunkered rulers, in vats; pragmatic vs visionary schism.
Clash at Kufra — battle at Libyan oasis; Ash Prophets, Ash Riders, Red Moon Cult, and Overseers’ drones. Massacre; Prophets embrace oblivion as return to serpent.

VII. Supplemental Lore (Appendices / Inserts)
Papa Samuel Ship Technicals — detailed technical dossier of the Papa Samuel, a Gemini Super-50 smuggler fortress. Lore insert written in technical-manual style, deliberately jarring against mythic flow.

VIII. Thematic Cycles (Meta-Layer)
Refrains:
“Not the comet. Not yet.”
“The serpent coils.”
“The coil turns again.”
“The forgetting begins.”

Cycle Pattern:
Civilization rises → hubris → collapse → blindness/catastrophe → subterranean survival → myths resurface → serpent returns.
