Style Sheet — The Magnolia Oubliette (Draft II)
1. General Style

Tone: Mythic, lyrical, recursive.
→ Avoid flattening into plain expository prose.
→ Preserve rhythm, repetition, and poetic phrasing.

Narrative voice: Third-person omniscient, with mythic detachment — almost like a chronicler or scribe.

Sentence structure: Long, flowing, recursive sentences are intentional.
→ Do not shorten unless clarity is lost.

2. Formatting Conventions

Chapter headings:
Preferred style:

The Magnolia Oubliette
Chapter X: Subtitle


but some are fragments labeled (installment one, two, three, four) — this is intentional for Chapter One.

Section breaks: “=======” or heavy horizontal dividers are deliberate.

Poetry, hymns, chants:

Set as indented stanzas or line-broken verse.

Preserve archaic repetition and fractured English.

Quoted fragments / graffiti: Often all-caps, short lines. Keep this style.

3. Key Terms & Spellings
Papulegara — always capitalized, never italicized.
The Custodians — capitalized; refers to the Neuralink pair.
The Dark — capitalized when referring to the resin.
Derinkuyu — underground city; spelling fixed.
The Obelisk — capitalized as a unique object.
The Overseers — capitalized; subterranean technocratic rulers.
Ash Prophets — capitalized cult.
The Citadel — capitalized when referring to Derinkuyu or the subterranean complex.
Not the comet. Not yet. — refrain; always in this exact form with periods, not commas.
The coil turns again. — refrain; keep lowercase except for “The.”

4. Motifs & Recurring Phrases

Repeated mythic refrains should remain exactly as written:
“Not the comet. Not yet.”
“The serpent coils.”
“The coil turns again.”
“The forgetting begins.”

Preserve recursive/echo patterns: “world without end,” “coil coil coil,” etc.

Preserve ambiguity in voices: HELLO LENA should remain in ALL CAPS on screens.

5. Special Sections

Papa Samuel Ship Technicals: written in technical-manual style, deliberately jarring against the mythic prose.
→ Do not “smooth” this into narrative — keep the stark difference.

Myth fragments (clay shard, graffiti, SCP-like fragments): Keep formatting shifts intact, they are intentional.

6. Capitalization Rules

Mythic objects/entities: capitalized (The Dark, The Citadel, The Custodians).

Natural forces/cycles: lowercase (the serpent, the coil, the tide), unless treated as proper name (Papulegara).

Chapter subtitles: Title Case.

7. Numbers & Dates
Keep spelled-out when mythic/historic (e.g., forty-thousand-year curse).
Use digits for data fragments/logs (e.g., BIRTHS SECTOR 2: 4).

8. Things Not To Change
Archaic phrasing: e.g., world without end, sowers of infinity.
Fragmented syntax: occasional sentence fragments are intentional, not errors.
Recursive metaphors: if a phrase repeats across chapters, it is deliberate.
Mix of registers: mythic + technical + modern slang (memes, hashtags) are part of the design. Do not “normalize” tone.
