Quick-Reference Glossary — The Magnolia Oubliette (Draft II)
Characters

Lena — Central POV in the citadel arc. Young woman of Derinkuyu. Experiences visions through The Dark resin. Possibly chosen as a “Navigator.”

Yoshihiro — Older, cunning, water-clan lineage. Charismatic manipulator. Guides Lena toward the Custodians/Obelisk. Ambiguous motives.

The Custodians — A man and a woman, last Neuralink pair. Arrived in an A-10 Warthog. Absorbed into the citadel’s systems, now a fused presence in wires, pools, Obelisk hum. Speak rarely, often via screens or pulses.

The Overseers — Post-collapse technocrats in global bunkers (e.g., beneath Geneva/CERN). Bodies sustained in nutrient vats, minds linked by implants. Rule by chords of thought. Ruthless pragmatists.

Groups & Factions

Derinkuyu / The Citadel — Underground Anatolian city. Refuge of survivors after Moon blindness. Organized by clans (water, air, agriculture, maintenance, security). Holds Obelisk chamber.

The Water Clan — Control cisterns and water distribution in Derinkuyu. Power faction; Yoshihiro is linked.

The Doorsmen — Enforcers; wield stone cudgels, enforce silence/taboos. Want breaches sealed.

The Bazaar Queen — Leader of commerce in Derinkuyu. Profiteer, tattoos glimmer with resin oil. Treats Custodians as spectacle.

Ash Prophets — Blind surface cult. Claim blindness is freedom and prophecy. Spread hymns and serpent worship. Nomadic caravans.

Ash Riders — Raider warband on bikes. Violent, soot-painted. Present at Kufra.

Red Moon Cult — Surface cult. Faces scarred/tattooed with crescents/spirals. Treat Moon as living god.

Surface scavengers & raiders — Post-blindness warbands, fragmented nations. Survival groups, often manipulated by Overseers.

Objects & Entities

Papulegara — The serpent of recursion. Mythic/metaphysical force. Sometimes dragon, sometimes coil, sometimes serpent. Returns in cycles (comet strikes, floods, blindness).

The Serpent — Symbol/metaphor recurring across myths and graffiti. Related to Papulegara. Recursion, ouroboros, self-devouring.

The Comet — Body in the Taurid swarm. Foreshadowed repeatedly: “Not the comet. Not yet.” Its strike (via Moon wound) triggers blindness/collapse.

The Moon’s Wound — Impact event that blinds billions. Leads to surface collapse. Dust veils sky.

The Obelisk — Black monolith in Derinkuyu. Emits hum, symbols, voices. Interface to Custodians/citadel. Speaks “HELLO LENA.”

The Dark (resin) — Bitter resin derived from hybrid magnolia trees + fungus. Smoked in bone pipes. Induces visions of serpent/Obelisk. Both key and chain. Currency + sacrament.

Resin Pipes — Bone or silver-inlaid smoking pipes. Used communally and ritually.

The Citadel — Sometimes means Derinkuyu entire; sometimes the deeper “living” structure fused with Custodians.

The A-10 Warthog — Old aircraft flown by the Custodians to Derinkuyu. Disassembled, parts fused into citadel systems.

Locations

Derinkuyu (Anatolia) — Primary underground setting. Labyrinth of clans, resin dens, and the Obelisk chamber.

Surface Ruins — Ash deserts, burned-out cities, famine lands. Populated by raiders, blind prophets, cults.

Geneva / CERN vaults — Overseer bunkers, vats, servers, chord-speech councils.

Kufra Oasis (Libya) — Battlefield of Ash Prophets, Riders, Red Moon Cult, Overseer drones. Scene of massacre.
